---
title: Property & Succession
summary: Title, partition, tenancy and possession disputes, and succession matters such as probate and succession certificates.
order: 4
---

The practice covers disputes and documentation relating to immovable property, including partition suits, title and possession disputes, tenancy and eviction matters, and drafting and review of agreements, sale deeds and leases.

It also handles succession matters, including applications for probate, letters of administration and succession certificates, and disputes over inheritance under the applicable personal and succession laws.
