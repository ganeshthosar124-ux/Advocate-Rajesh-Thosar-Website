The practice handles disputes relating to immovable property and succession, together with the drafting and review of documents connected with property.

## Matters in this area

- Partition suits and disputes over family property
- Title and possession disputes, and suits for injunction
- Tenancy and eviction matters
- Drafting and review of agreements, sale deeds and leases
- Applications for probate, letters of administration and succession certificates
- Disputes over inheritance under the applicable personal and succession laws
