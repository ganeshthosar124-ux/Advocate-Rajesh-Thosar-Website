The practice handles matrimonial and family matters before Family Courts and Magistrate courts.

## Matters in this area

- Petitions for divorce, both contested and by mutual consent
- Judicial separation and restitution of conjugal rights
- Maintenance proceedings
- Custody and guardianship of children
- Proceedings under the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005

Where appropriate, parties may also be informed of the mediation and settlement options available through the courts.
