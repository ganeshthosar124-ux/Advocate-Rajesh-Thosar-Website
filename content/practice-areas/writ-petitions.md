The practice handles writ petitions before the Bombay High Court under Articles 226 and 227 of the Constitution of India.

## Matters in this area

- Writ petitions under Article 226 challenging actions and orders of government departments and public authorities
- Petitions under Article 227 against orders of subordinate courts and tribunals
- Challenges to orders of statutory and regulatory authorities
