---
title: Cheque Dishonour
summary: Complaints and defence in cheque dishonour cases under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act.
order: 3
---

The practice handles matters relating to dishonour of cheques under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881, for complainants and accused persons. Work includes issuing and replying to statutory demand notices, filing and defending complaints before Magistrate courts, and related revisions and appeals.
