The practice appears in criminal matters before Judicial Magistrate First Class (JMFC) and Chief Judicial Magistrate (CJM) courts, Sessions Courts and the Bombay High Court.

## Matters in this area

- Bail and anticipatory bail applications
- Trials before Magistrate and Sessions Courts
- Private criminal complaints
- Criminal revision applications and appeals
- Petitions before the Bombay High Court for quashing of proceedings

## A note on the new criminal laws

Criminal procedure is now governed by the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023, and offences by the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, which came into force on 1 July 2024. Matters that arose before that date may continue under the earlier Code of Criminal Procedure and Indian Penal Code.
