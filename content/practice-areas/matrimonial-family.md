---
title: Matrimonial & Family Law
summary: Divorce, maintenance, custody and guardianship, and domestic violence proceedings.
order: 7
---

The practice handles matrimonial and family matters before Family Courts and Magistrate courts, including petitions for divorce and judicial separation, restitution of conjugal rights, maintenance, custody and guardianship of children, and proceedings under the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005.

Where appropriate, parties may also be informed of mediation and settlement options available through the courts.
