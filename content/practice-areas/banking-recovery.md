The practice appears in matters before the Debts Recovery Tribunal, including applications and appeals relating to proceedings under the Securitisation and Reconstruction of Financial Assets and Enforcement of Security Interest (SARFAESI) Act, 2002, and the Recovery of Debts and Bankruptcy Act, 1993.
