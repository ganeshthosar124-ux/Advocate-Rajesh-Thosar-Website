The practice handles banking and financial recovery matters, including proceedings before the Debts Recovery Tribunal and civil recovery suits.

## Matters in this area

- Proceedings under the Securitisation and Reconstruction of Financial Assets and Enforcement of Security Interest (SARFAESI) Act, 2002
- Applications and appeals before the Debts Recovery Tribunal under the Recovery of Debts and Bankruptcy Act, 1993
- Civil recovery suits, including summary suits
- Replies to notices issued by banks and financial institutions
