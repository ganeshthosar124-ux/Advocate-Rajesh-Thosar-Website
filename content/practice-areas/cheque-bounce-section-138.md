The practice handles matters relating to the dishonour of cheques under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881, acting for complainants as well as for persons accused in such complaints.

## Matters in this area

- Drafting and sending statutory demand notices, and replies to notices received
- Filing complaints before the Judicial Magistrate First Class
- Defence of persons accused in cheque dishonour complaints
- Revisions and appeals arising from these cases

## How a Section 138 case generally proceeds

1. The cheque is returned unpaid by the bank, for example for insufficient funds.
2. A written demand notice is sent to the person who issued the cheque within 30 days of receiving the bank's information about its return.
3. The person who issued the cheque has 15 days from receiving the notice to make payment.
4. If payment is not made, a complaint may be filed before the Magistrate within one month after those 15 days end.

These time limits are set by the Act, and whether they are met depends on the facts of each case.
