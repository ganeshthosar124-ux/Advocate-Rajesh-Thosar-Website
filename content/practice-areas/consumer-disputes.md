The practice handles complaints and appeals under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, before the District, State and National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commissions (commonly called consumer courts).

## Matters in this area

- Complaints about deficiency in service or defective goods
- Disputes over insurance claims
- Disputes with builders and developers over housing and real estate
- Appeals before the State and National Commissions

Which Commission hears a complaint depends on the value of the goods or services paid for.
