The practice handles civil disputes before civil courts at the trial and appellate stages, from the drafting of pleadings through to hearings and the execution of decrees.

## Matters in this area

- Suits for recovery of money
- Suits for specific performance of contracts
- Suits for declaration and injunction, including applications for temporary injunctions
- First and second appeals, and civil revision applications
- Execution proceedings for decrees and orders
- Drafting of plaints, written statements, applications, affidavits and written submissions

Work on each matter typically begins with a review of the documents and the facts, followed by research on the applicable law and procedure before the pleadings are settled.
