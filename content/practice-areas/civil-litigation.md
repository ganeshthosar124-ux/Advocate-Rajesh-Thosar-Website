---
title: Civil Litigation
summary: Suits, injunctions, recovery proceedings, appeals and execution before civil courts.
order: 1
---

The practice handles civil disputes before civil courts at the trial and appellate stages. Matters include suits for recovery of money, specific performance of contracts, declarations and injunctions, as well as appeals, revisions and execution proceedings.

Work in each matter typically involves review of documents, drafting of pleadings, applications and written submissions, and appearance at hearings.
